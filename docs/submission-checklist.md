# Submission checklist

Draft submissions for editorial galleries and awards. **Nothing has been submitted, no fee has
been paid, and no account has been created.** Every item requires Mohammed's explicit approval,
and several cost money.

## Standing rules

- Never claim an award before winning it.
- Never display an award badge or logo without the awarding body's permission.
- Never submit a site that still shows concept compositions as if they were screenshots.
- Never enter a paid competition without Mohammed's explicit approval of the fee.
- Submit only after `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` is set and the site is live and stable.

---

## Readiness gate

Do not submit anywhere until all are true:

- [ ] Live on the real domain, stable for at least a week
- [ ] Real product screenshots in place (galleries judge visual credibility hardest)
- [ ] Contact form verified end to end with a real provider
- [ ] Lighthouse run on the live site with scores Mohammed is happy to be judged on
- [ ] Arabic proofread by a native reader
- [ ] Every award claim on the site is accurate and describable as a **team** award

## Developer and design communities

| Community | Angle |
|---|---|
| **Hacker News** (Show HN) | Lead with the *engineering* story — bilingual RTL as a structural problem — not "check out my portfolio" |
| **r/web_design**, **r/webdev** | Ask for critique; these communities reward honesty and punish self-promotion |
| **Designer Hangout / Slack communities** | Feedback before wider submission |
| **Dev.to / Hashnode** | Publish the Arabic-first article from `launch-plan.md` week 3 |
| **Iraqi & regional tech communities** | The most likely source of actual clients |

## Suggested order

1. Free galleries + communities → collect feedback, fix what surfaces.
2. Fix the biggest weakness (almost certainly real screenshots).
3. Only then consider a paid award submission — and only with approval of the fee.
